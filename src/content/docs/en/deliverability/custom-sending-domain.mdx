In this guide you'll review your default sender and add a custom domain in Nevent so your campaign emails arrive from your own domain, for example `email.yourfestival.com`, instead of Nevent's shared domain.

This improves deliverability (more emails reach the inbox instead of spam) and reinforces your brand's image with your contacts.

Adding the domain takes a few minutes. DNS verification can take anywhere from a few hours to 72 hours, depending on your domain provider.

## Before you start

You need three things:

- To know your root domain, for example `yourfestival.com`. It's the same one you use for your website.
- Access to your domain provider's DNS panel (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, your hosting provider's...). That's where you'll create the records Nevent shows you when you add the domain. If you don't have that access, ask whoever manages your website to do it for you.
- Your organization's legal details filled in, including the postal address. You'll complete them in step 4.

:::note[Quick glossary]
- **Domain**: your website's address, like `yourfestival.com`.
- **Subdomain**: a prefix in front of your domain, like `email.yourfestival.com`. You'll use it exclusively for Nevent's sends.
- **DNS record**: an instruction that tells the internet how to handle your domain. You'll create these in your domain provider's panel.
:::

## Step 1: open Senders and domains

In the side menu, scroll down to the bottom and click **Settings** (the last item in the menu, with the gear icon).

Inside Settings, find the **Workspace** group in the left-hand secondary menu. Click **Senders and domains**.

This screen used to be called "Custom domain". Here you see the name and address your emails arrive from and where replies go.

![Senders and domains screen of DEMO FEST with the Default sender card, the red warning about replies not reaching you and, below, the Senders and Domains lists](/images/guias/entregabilidad/dominio-dedicado/paso-1-remitentes-y-dominios.png)

## Step 2: review your default sender

At the top is the **Default sender** card. It shows the **Sender name**, the **Sender email** and the **Reply-to address**.

On the right of the card you see its status, for example **Action required**. Below, a sentence tells you where your emails come from right now. If you don't have your own domain yet, they go out from a Nevent subdomain reserved for your organization.

:::note[Note]
If the status says **Can't send**, the sender can't send emails yet. Read the warning on the card: it tells you what's happening and what to do.
:::

## Step 3: change the reply-to address if replies don't reach you

If the reply-to address can't receive email, the warning **You're not receiving replies to your emails** appears. When someone replies, the reply goes to that address and doesn't reach you.

Click **Change reply-to address**. A dialog opens on the same page.

Type an address someone reads, for example `hello@yourfestival.com`, and click **Save**. If you don't want to change anything, click **Cancel**.

![Change reply-to address dialog with the empty Reply-to address field, the help text and the Cancel and Save buttons](/images/guias/entregabilidad/dominio-dedicado/paso-3-dialogo-respuesta.png)

If your sender is already active, Nevent may first ask for a test email to confirm that the address receives mail. Follow the steps the dialog shows.

## Step 4: complete your legal details

To apply a new sender with your domain, Nevent needs your organization's legal name and postal address.

In the secondary menu, inside the **Account & plan** group, click **Legal**. Fill in the **Address** field (it's required; a blank one isn't valid) and click **Save** at the top right.

![Legal screen with the required Address field and its help text: Your organization's postal address. It's needed to apply a new sender with your domain](/images/guias/entregabilidad/dominio-dedicado/paso-4-legal-direccion.png)

:::note[Note]
If legal details are missing, Nevent's warning names the missing fields with the same labels you see in **Settings › Legal**.
:::

## Step 5: add your domain

Go back to **Settings › Senders and domains** and, in the **Domains** section, click **Add domain**.

Type your brand's domain in **Domain**, without `www` or subdomains. For example: `yourfestival.com`.

The **Bounce domain** field receives bounce notices and Nevent already suggests one for you. Change it only if whoever manages your domain asks you to. It's never used as a sender.

Click **Check domain**. Nevent first reads your domain without changing anything and then registers it.

![Add domain screen with the Domain field, the Bounce domain field with the value bounce and the Check domain button](/images/guias/entregabilidad/dominio-dedicado/paso-2-anadir-dominio.png)

:::caution[Important]
Click **Check domain** only once. One click is enough.
:::

## Step 6: Create the DNS records with your provider

After adding the domain, Nevent shows the DNS records to publish. Here you have all the values you need to copy and paste into your domain provider's panel.

You'll see the records grouped into these sections:

| Record type | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **DKIM** (one, two or three CNAME-type records) | A digital signature that proves the email was sent by you and not a third party |
| **SPF** (a TXT-type record) | A list of servers authorized to send on behalf of your domain |
| **Bounce domain** (MX and TXT records) | Receives bounce notices; improves domain reputation |
| **DMARC** (a TXT-type record) | A policy telling email providers what to do if an email fails SPF or DKIM |

:::note[Record glossary]
- **CNAME**: an alias that points to another domain.
- **TXT**: free text that mail servers read to verify permissions.
- **MX**: indicates which servers handle a domain's mail.
:::

For each record, copy the **Name**, **Type** and **Value** fields using the copy icon next to each one. Then go to your domain provider's DNS panel and create each record with those values.

:::tip[Tip]
Open your provider's panel in a new window and keep this Nevent screen open in another. Copy and paste each record one at a time to avoid mistakes.
:::

:::caution[Important]
DKIM values are long strings of text. Don't transcribe them by hand: always use the copy button to avoid errors.
:::

## Step 7: Verify the record status

Once you've created every record with your domain provider, go back to Nevent and click the **Verify status** button.

Nevent checks whether the records are already visible on the internet. Depending on your provider, propagation can take anywhere from a few minutes to 72 hours. If any record shows as "Pending" when you check, wait a few hours and try again.

Once every record is verified, your custom domain will become active and your next email campaigns will send from `email.yourfestival.com`.

:::note[Note]
You don't have to wait for everything to be verified before closing the screen. You can leave and come back later to check the status.
:::

## Result

Your custom domain is now added in Nevent. In **Senders and domains** you'll see its status: **Not verified** until the records are verified and **Verified** afterwards. From now on, your contacts will see your own domain as the sender in their inboxes, which improves the delivery rate and reinforces trust in your communications.

:::tip[Tip]
If, after 72 hours, a record is still "Pending", check in your provider's panel that you copied the name and value exactly as Nevent shows them. An extra space or an incorrect character can prevent verification.
:::
