In this guide you'll walk through the **Channels** section of Nevent's Settings. You'll see which channels you have available, what state each one is in, and what you need to do to activate the ones that aren't running yet. About 5 minutes.

## Before you start

You need access to the admin panel with administrator or manager permissions in your organization. If you don't see the Settings option in the side menu, contact whoever gave you access to the account.

## Step 1: Access Settings and open the Channels section

In the side menu, scroll down to the bottom and click **Settings** (the last item in the menu, with the gear icon).

Inside Settings, find the **Brand and Content** group in the left-hand secondary menu. Click **Channels**.

The "Communication Channels" screen opens with four tabs: **Email**, **SMS**, **WhatsApp** and **Analytics**.

## Step 2: The email channel

Email is the primary channel and comes activated by default on every Nevent account. You don't need to do anything to start sending email campaigns.

On this tab you can adjust three things:

- **CC recipients:** add addresses that will receive a copy of every email campaign you send.
- **Sender:** the name the recipient sees in the "From" field of their inbox. If you leave it empty, it uses your organization's name.
- **Default reply-to address:** where your contacts' replies go. You can override it campaign by campaign.
- **Email signature:** the block of text and colors that appears at the bottom of all your emails. It includes legal information, social media links and the unsubscribe link.

:::tip[Tip]
Always fill in the reply-to address with an inbox you check daily. When someone replies to a campaign email, it's a sign of real interest: don't miss those messages.
:::

## Step 3: The SMS channel

SMS lets you send text messages directly to your contacts' phones. It's the channel with the highest open rate, though it requires SMS credits and some extra configuration before you can send.

Click the **SMS** tab.

You'll see an important notice about the CNMC Alias Registry (the Comisión Nacional de los Mercados y la Competencia, Spain's telecommunications regulator). Starting September 15, 2026, operators will block SMS whose sender isn't registered there. Nevent handles this process for you: contact the support team to get it started.

To enable the channel:

1. Turn on the **Enable SMS** switch.
2. Add at least one **Sender ID**: this is the name, up to 11 characters, that will appear as the sender on the recipient's phone (for example, MYVENUE or FEST2026).
3. Save your changes with the **Save** button in the top-right corner.

:::caution[Important]
If you don't register your Sender IDs with the CNMC before September 15, 2026, operators will block your messages. Nevent handles the registration: contact support to start the process as soon as possible.
:::

## Step 4: The WhatsApp channel

WhatsApp lets you send marketing campaigns directly to your contacts' messaging app. To activate it, you need to connect a WhatsApp Business account, which is Meta's official business profile.

Click the **WhatsApp** tab.

You'll see the connection screen with the **Connect WhatsApp Business** button. Clicking it opens Meta's verification flow, where you authorize Nevent to send messages on behalf of your business account.

This process is handled directly by Meta: you'll need access to the phone number you use as your WhatsApp Business number and to your Meta (Facebook) business account.

:::note[Note]
Once WhatsApp Business is connected, you'll be able to create and send message templates from the WhatsApp Templates section. Templates need Meta's approval before they can be used in campaigns.
:::

## Step 5: The Analytics channel

The **Analytics** tab isn't a communication channel as such, but rather the connection to external measurement tools. Here you connect your campaign tracking with Meta and Google.

Click the **Analytics** tab.

You'll see two integrations:

- **Meta Pixel:** enter your pixel's numeric ID (you'll find it in Meta's ad manager, under "Data sources"). Once activated, Nevent automatically sends conversion events to Meta, which improves the performance of your paid campaigns on Facebook and Instagram.
- **Google Analytics:** enter your GA4 property's measurement ID (in the format G-XXXXXXXXXX). Once activated, Nevent sends your campaign data to Google Analytics so you can see traffic and conversions in your GA4 reports.

Turn on each integration's switch only once you've entered the correct ID. Then click **Save**.

## Result

You now know Nevent's four communication channels and how each one is configured. Email is ready to use from day one. SMS and WhatsApp require extra configuration and registration steps before you can send. Analytics lets you connect Meta and Google to enrich your campaign data.

:::tip[Tip]
You don't have to activate every channel at once. Start with email, which requires no configuration, and add SMS or WhatsApp when you want to expand your reach.
:::
