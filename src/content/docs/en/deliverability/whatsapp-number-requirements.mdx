This page gathers, as a quick reference, the technical requirements, the possible statuses and the messaging limits that apply when you connect a WhatsApp Business number in Nevent. The limit and eligibility data come from Meta's official documentation for the WhatsApp Business Platform.

## Number requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Number type | With country and area code (short codes are not accepted). It can be a mobile or a landline. |
| Ownership | The number must belong to you or your business. |
| Ability to receive | It must be able to receive voice calls or SMS, to complete verification. |
| Previous use on WhatsApp | It cannot be registered at that moment in the WhatsApp app or the WhatsApp Business app, unless you turn on coexistence (see below) or delete that app's account first. |
| Verification | By SMS or by voice call. Landlines can only be verified by voice call. |
| Numbers per account | 2 numbers to start with. It rises automatically to 20 when the business is verified or when a number reaches a messaging tier of 2,000 conversations. |

## Coexistence: a number shared between the app and the API

Meta lets you use the same phone number at the same time in the WhatsApp Business app (for manual, one-to-one conversations) and on the API Nevent uses (for campaigns and automations). Messages from both channels stay in sync in the history.

Some points to keep in mind:

- It requires a recent version of the WhatsApp Business app (2.24.17 or later).
- A number in coexistence has a fixed technical sending limit of 20 messages per second, to keep compatibility with the app.
- Messages sent from the app are still free. Messages sent through the API (those of your Nevent campaigns) are billed at Meta's rates.
- Coexistence for your account is turned on by the Nevent support team.

For a complete explanation of what is kept, which requirements apply and when it suits you or not, see [What WhatsApp coexistence mode is and when it suits you](/en/deliverability/whatsapp-coexistence-mode/).

## Account and number statuses in Meta

| Status | Where you see it | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Business verified | Business Manager > Business verification | Meta has checked your business's legal identity. It is a requirement to raise the number quota and for some eligibilities. |
| Business in review / rejected / not started | Business Manager > Business verification | Verification has not been completed yet or Meta has rejected it. Check the documents you provided. |
| Display name approved / pending / rejected | WhatsApp Manager > Business profiles | Shows whether the name your contacts will see as the sender can already be used. |
| Number connected (Connected) | WhatsApp Manager > Phone numbers | The number is active and can send and receive messages through the API. |
| Number quality: green (GREEN) | WhatsApp Manager > Phone numbers | High quality: few or no negative signals from users. |
| Number quality: yellow (YELLOW) | WhatsApp Manager > Phone numbers | Medium quality: it has received negative signals or a low read rate; it may end up paused if it does not improve. |
| Number quality: red (RED) | WhatsApp Manager > Phone numbers | Low quality: high risk that Meta lowers your messaging limit. |
| Number quality: no data (NA / UNKNOWN) | WhatsApp Manager > Phone numbers | There are not enough sends or user signals yet to calculate the quality. |

## Messaging tiers (Meta)

The messaging tier is the maximum number of unique recipients you can message outside a conversation opened by the user, in a rolling 24-hour window. It is calculated per business account (business portfolio), not per number: if you have several numbers in the same account, they share the same quota.

| Tier | Unique recipients in 24 h | How you reach it |
|---|---|---|
| Initial | 250 | The starting tier of every new account. |
| Expanded | 2,000 | By verifying the business, having a partner verify it, or sending 2,000 messages delivered to unique recipients in a rolling 30-day window with high-quality templates. |
| Higher | 10,000 | Automatic upgrade by Meta if you keep high quality and use at least half of your current quota in the last 7 days. |
| High volume | 100,000 | Automatic upgrade by Meta with the same quality and usage criteria. |
| Unlimited | Unlimited | Automatic upgrade for accounts with a sustained history of high quality and volume. |

See exactly why these tiers apply in [WhatsApp messaging limits: why it stops at 40](/en/deliverability/whatsapp-messaging-limits/).

## How each message is billed

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| What is billed | Since July 1, 2025, Meta charges per message, not per conversation: each template message you send is billed at Meta's rate for the recipient's country and the template's category (marketing, utility or authentication). Free-form replies inside the 24-hour customer service window are service messages: from October 1, 2026, each number gets 1,000 free per month and the rest are charged. |
| Cost in Nevent | WhatsApp does not use credits at a flat rate like SMS or email. It is billed at Meta's per-message rates, not with the plan's credits. For the credits included in your plan, ask the Nevent team. |
