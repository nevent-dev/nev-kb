A lookalike audience lets you advertise to new people who resemble your real buyers, instead of guessing who might be interested. If you also exclude those same buyers from the campaign, your acquisition budget is not wasted on people who already know you.

You do this guide in **Meta Ads Manager**, starting from a custom audience you have already synced from Nevent. About 10 minutes.

## Before you start

- **A segment already synced as a custom audience in Meta.** If you do not have one yet, first follow [how to sync a segment as an audience](/en/paid-media/sync-a-segment-as-an-audience/).
- **The admin or advertiser role** on the Meta ad account, needed to create a lookalike audience from a custom audience. *Source: Meta Business Help Center, "Create a Lookalike Audience".*
- **A minimum size for your source audience.** Meta requires at least 100 people in the source and recommends between 1,000 and 5,000 for a good result. *Source: Meta Business Help Center, "Create a Lookalike Audience".*

## Step 1: Go to Audiences in Meta Ads Manager

In **Meta Ads Manager**, open the tools menu and go to **Audiences**.

Find in the list the custom audience you synced from Nevent. You recognize it by the name of the segment you gave it in Nevent.

## Step 2: Create a lookalike audience from that audience

Click **Create audience** and choose **Lookalike audience**.

As the source, select the custom audience synced from Nevent that you found in the previous step.

## Step 3: Choose the country and the percentage

Select the country or area where you want Meta to look for those new people.

Next, move the percentage slider, between 1% and 10%. This value decides how much the new audience resembles your source audience:

- The **lower** the percentage, the more the new audience resembles your source audience, but the smaller it is.
- The **higher** the percentage, the more reach you get, but the resemblance to your source audience drops.

For events, starting with a low percentage and raising it later if you need more reach is usually the safest choice.

Confirm with **Create audience**. Meta takes up to three days to finish building it, although you can use it in a campaign right away.

## Step 4: Use the lookalike in your ad set and exclude the source audience

When you create or edit an ad set, in the **Audience** section:

1. Add the lookalike audience you just created as the ad set's audience.
2. In the **Exclusions** section, add the source custom audience (the one you synced from Nevent).

With this, the acquisition ad is shown to new people who resemble your buyers, without repeating it to those who are already in your source audience.

## What to check

- **Estimated size of the lookalike audience.** If it shows far below what you would expect for your country, wait until it finishes populating or check that the source audience has enough volume.
- **That the source audience is still synced.** Check it in the Nevent segment: the **Activate as an advertising audience** block should still show the **Connected** status for Meta.
- **Your plan's limits.** How many lookalike and synced audiences your plan allows is confirmed by the Nevent team.

:::note[Note]
The lookalike audience is always created and managed in Meta, not in Nevent. Nevent's role is to keep the source audience up to date: if you change the segment's conditions or new buyers come in, sync it again so the lookalike is built on current data.
:::
