Connecting your Meta, Google and TikTok accounts is the first thing you need to do so the Paid Media module in Nevent can show the spend and performance of your ads in a single panel. The process is a standard authorization (OAuth): you approve access from your own account, without installing anything.

In this guide you connect all three platforms. Each one has its own card and its own button, so you can connect them all in a row or just the one you use. Count on about 10 minutes if you're connecting all three, or about 3 minutes for each one if you connect them separately.

## Before you start

You need enough permissions on each platform to authorize access. Check them before you start:

- **Meta (Facebook and Instagram Ads):** you need the **admin** or **advertiser** role on the ad account inside your Business Manager. *Source: Meta Business Help Center, "What are the ad account permission roles in Meta Ads Manager?".*
- **Google Ads:** you need **Standard** or **Admin** access level on the Google Ads account. Read-only access does not let you complete the authorization. *Source: Google Ads Help, "About access levels in your Google Ads account".*
- **TikTok Ads:** you need a role that lets you manage ads in the TikTok Business Center.

If you don't have any of these permissions, ask whoever manages that account at your company before continuing.

## Step 1: go to Settings → Paid Ads

In the side menu of Nevent, go to **Settings** and click **Paid Ads**.

You'll see a card for each platform: Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and Google Ads. Each card is independent, with its own button and its own status: **Not connected** with a **Connect** button, **Connected** with the account name, or **Error** with a **Retry** button.

{/* SCREENSHOT: Settings → Paid Ads screen in Nevent with the three cards (Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Google Ads) in "Not connected" status and their "Connect" button */}

## Step 2: connect your Meta account

Click **Connect** on the **Meta Ads** card. Nevent takes you to Meta's authorization screen, where you sign in with your personal Facebook account and choose the Business Manager and the ad account you want to share with Nevent.

{/* SCREENSHOT: Meta authorization screen asking you to sign in and choose the Business Manager and ad account to share with Nevent */}

After accepting, you go back to **Settings → Paid Ads** and the **Meta Ads** card now shows the **Connected** status.

:::note[Note]
If at any point in the process Meta's **Ads Manager** opens in another tab and asks you to sign in to Instagram with your personal account, that's normal Meta behavior when you enter its platform directly. It's not part of the authorization with Nevent and it doesn't affect the connection.
:::

## Step 3: connect your Google Ads account

In **Settings → Paid Ads**, click **Connect** on the **Google Ads** card.

Sign in with your Google account and select the Google Ads account you want to share with Nevent.

{/* SCREENSHOT: Google Ads account selector inside the connection wizard, showing the available accounts to choose from */}

## Step 4: connect your TikTok Ads account

In **Settings → Paid Ads**, click **Connect** on the **TikTok Ads** card. Sign in with your TikTok Business Center account and authorize access to the ad account you want to analyze in Nevent.

{/* SCREENSHOT: TikTok Ads card in Settings → Paid Ads after completing the authorization, showing the "Connected" status */}

## What to check

- Each platform's card should show the **Connected** status, with the ad account name and the date of the last sync.
- If you have several ad accounts, one per profile or brand, confirm that the one showing as connected is the one that manages the event you want to analyze.
- If Meta doesn't show as connected after authorizing, check your role in the Business Manager, confirm you selected the correct ad account during authorization, and click **Connect** again to repeat the process.
- To see the detail of your campaigns once connected, follow [how to read a campaign, ad set and ad detail](/en/paid-media/how-to-read-a-campaign-ad-set-and-ad/).
