Get Nevent running in three steps: connect your ticketing provider, pick the goal that matters most to you right now and reach your first real result. About 15 minutes to the first result, depending on the goal you pick.

## Step 1: Connect your ticketing provider

Everything else depends on this step: as soon as you connect your ticketing provider, your fans and their purchases come into Nevent on their own, without uploading any file.

<LinkCard
  title="Connect your ticketing platform to Nevent"
  description="In 3 minutes your fans come in on their own. The step that unlocks everything else."
  href="/en/audience/connect-your-ticketing-platform/"
/>

<Aside type="tip" title="Is your ticketing provider not integrated?">
  See [what to do if your ticketing provider is not on the list](/en/audience/ticketing-platform-not-integrated/). You can still [import your contacts](/en/audience/import-your-contacts/) to get started.
</Aside>
