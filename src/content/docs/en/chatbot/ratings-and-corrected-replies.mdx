Your fans can tell you whether the chatbot helped them: rate each reply, answer whether their question was resolved and rate the team when a person looks after them. With those signals you see in **Performance** how conversations end, and from the **Inbox** you correct the replies that went wrong so the assistant doesn't repeat the mistake.

In this guide you turn on ratings, check the results and correct a reply. About 10 minutes.

## Step 1: Turn on ratings in each channel

Ratings are set channel by channel. In the side menu, under **Chatbot**, open the channel you want (**Web channel**, **WhatsApp channel** or **Instagram channel**) and look for the **Ratings** card.

In the **Web channel** you have three options:

- **Let fans rate each reply**: the fan can mark each reply with a thumbs up or down.
- **Ask whether the question was resolved**: after 2 minutes without a message, once per conversation.
- **Ask for a team rating**: when your team resolves a conversation.

![The "Valoraciones" (Ratings) card in the Demo Fest Web channel, with the text that ratings show in Performance and in the Inbox, the three switches turned on for rating each reply, asking whether the question was resolved and asking for a team rating, and the Discard and Save changes buttons.](/images/guias/chatbot/valoraciones-y-respuestas-corregidas/paso-1-valoraciones-canal-web.png)

How changes are saved depends on the channel:

- **Web channel**: turn on or off whatever you want and click **Save changes**. **Discard** takes you back to what you had.
- **WhatsApp channel**: each switch is saved as soon as you touch it. In WhatsApp, Meta counts each rating question as another reply.
- **Instagram channel**: ratings are saved with the same button as the ice breakers.

## Step 2: Check resolution in Performance

In **Chatbot › Performance**, below **Sales push**, is the **Resolution** block: how conversations end and what your fans think. It uses the same date range and channel filters as the rest of the screen.

![The "Resolución" (Resolution) block in Demo Fest Performance with 4 finished conversations: 100% · 4 resolved by the assistant (0 confirmed by fans · 4 based on automatic review), 0% · 0 handled by the team and 0% · 0 unresolved, and below it the Rated replies and Team ratings cards, both with no ratings yet.](/images/guias/chatbot/valoraciones-y-respuestas-corregidas/paso-2-resolucion-rendimiento.png)

What you see:

- **Finished conversations**, split into **Resolved by the assistant**, **Handled by the team** and **Unresolved**. For those resolved by the assistant, it shows how many were **confirmed by fans** and how many were classified **based on automatic review**.
- **Rated replies**: your fans' thumbs, positive and negative. It only appears for web chat; it is hidden for WhatsApp and Instagram.
- **Team ratings**: what fans score when a person looks after them (**Good**, **Fair** or **Poor**).
- **Reasons for negative reply ratings**: a table with the reason for each poorly rated reply (for example **Incorrect**, **Outdated**, **Missed the question**, **Did not know** or **Asked for a person**) and whether it comes from a thumbs down or from **Automatic review**.

If there are conversations waiting for review, Performance links you to them in the Inbox.

## Step 3: Review conversations in the Inbox

In **Chatbot › Inbox**, the **To review** view gathers the conversations worth looking at, with their reason. The number next to the view tells you how many are pending.

![The Demo Fest Inbox with the views All, Mine, Unassigned, In progress, Resolved and To review (0), the channel filters and the list of conversations.](/images/guias/chatbot/valoraciones-y-respuestas-corregidas/paso-3-bandeja-por-revisar.png)

When you open a conversation, the details show the signals it left: whether the fan answered "No", the team rating, or whether the question was resolved according to the fan. Web chat ratings appear next to the rated reply. From there you have two actions:

- **Mark as reviewed**, when you've looked at it and there's nothing to correct.
- **Correct reply**, from the assistant message that went wrong.

When you click **Correct reply** you see the fan's **Question** and the **Answer** the assistant gave. Write the **Correct answer** and click **Save correction**. The assistant will use it for similar questions.

This guide has no screenshot of the correction step because the demo organization had no conversations to review on the day it was written.

## Step 4: Manage corrected replies

All your team's corrections are in **Chatbot › Settings**, in the **Corrected replies** tab. From there you can edit each correction or delete it. When you delete one, Nevent asks you to confirm, because the assistant will stop using it.

The **Assistant status** panel counts how many corrected replies you have.

![The "Respuestas corregidas" (Corrected replies) tab of the Demo Fest chatbot settings, with the text that these are the replies your team corrects from the Inbox and that there are none yet, and the Assistant status panel showing no corrected replies.](/images/guias/chatbot/valoraciones-y-respuestas-corregidas/paso-4-respuestas-corregidas.png)

## Result

Your fans rate the chatbot in the channels you chose, you see in Performance how conversations end, and every correction you save improves the assistant's replies to similar questions.
