You can use the Nevent dashboard in **Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Italian or German**, and choose how dates, times and numbers are shown. Both settings are saved to your account: you will see them the same in any browser.

In this guide you change the dashboard language and its regional format. About 2 minutes.

## Step 1: Open your user panel

Click your initial at the top right. Your user panel opens with your plan, plan usage and quick access.

At the bottom of the panel are the **Language** and **Regional format** drop-downs.

![User panel for Demo open over the Integrations screen, with the Enterprise plan, plan usage, quick access and, at the bottom, the Language (Español) and Regional format (Automático) drop-downs with the example 31/10/26 · 21:30 · 1.234.567,891.](/images/guias/organizacion/idioma-y-formato-regional/paso-1-panel-de-usuario.png)

## Step 2: Choose the language

Open the **Language** drop-down and choose one from the list: **Español**, **English**, **Português**, **Français**, **Italiano** or **Deutsch**.

![Language drop-down open in the user panel with the options Español (selected), English, Português, Français and Italiano.](/images/guias/organizacion/idioma-y-formato-regional/paso-2-lista-de-idiomas.png)

The dashboard switches language as soon as you choose one. You don't need to save or reload the page.

:::note[Note]
If you have never chosen a language, Nevent uses your browser's language when it is one of the six available, and English otherwise.
:::

## Step 3: Choose the regional format

In **Regional format** you decide how dates, times and numbers are written. The options are:

- **Automatic**
- **Spanish (Spain)** and **Spanish (Mexico)**
- **Portuguese (Portugal)**
- **English (United Kingdom)** and **English (United States)**
- **French (France)**
- **Italian (Italy)**
- **German (Germany)**

Below the drop-down you'll see an example in the chosen format, such as `31/10/2026 · 21:30 · 1,234,567.891`, so you can check it before you carry on working.

## Result

The dashboard is shown in the language and regional format you chose. Because it's saved to your account, you'll see it the same if you sign in from another computer or another browser.

:::tip[Tip]
The language and regional format are yours: they don't change what the rest of your team sees or the language of the messages your fans receive.
:::
