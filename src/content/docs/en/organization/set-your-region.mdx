In **Settings › Region** you decide how Nevent adapts to where you work: the time zone reports use to count days and "today", the currency they will be shown in, and the language of the texts Nevent adds to your messages.

## Step 1: Open the Region screen

In the side menu, click **Settings** and, under **Workspace**, click **Region**.

The screen shows a single card with four fields. Each one has a line underneath that explains what it is for.

![The "Region" screen for Demo Fest with four fields: Country of operation (Spain), Time zone (Madrid GMT+1), Reporting currency (Euro EUR) and Communication language (Spanish, locked, with the note "Proposed for Spain"). At the bottom, the Reset and Save buttons.](/images/guias/organizacion/configura-la-region/paso-1-pantalla-region.png)

## Step 2: Review each field

- **Country of operation.** Nevent uses it to propose the currency and language, and to decide which time zones you can choose. The tax country is not changed here: it is managed in the **Billing center**.
- **Time zone.** Reports and analytics count days, months and "today" in it. Zones are listed by city or region name, for example **Madrid (GMT+1)**.
- **Reporting currency.** The currency you will see reports in. The screen itself notes that it will apply once reports in your currency are turned on.
- **Communication language.** The language of the texts Nevent adds to your messages, such as the legal footer and the unsubscribe page. If the field is locked, ask Nevent to turn on language changes for your account to change it.

Under each value, a discreet line tells you where it comes from: for example, **Proposed for Spain** when it is Nevent's proposal for your country.

## Step 3: Change what you need and check the warnings

When you choose a different value, the field shows **Current value** with the previous one and an **Undo** link to go back to it. At the bottom of the card, **You have unsaved changes** appears.

If you change the time zone, a warning under the field lists what will change when you save: the organizations that inherit that zone, which change too, and reports and analytics, which change too, past days included.

![The "Region" screen with the time zone changed to Canarias (GMT) and not yet saved. Under the field it reads "Current value: Madrid (GMT+1)" with the Undo link, and a yellow warning lists the organizations that inherit the zone and notes that reports and analytics change too, past days included. At the bottom, "You have unsaved changes" with the Reset and Save buttons.](/images/guias/organizacion/configura-la-region/paso-2-cambios-sin-guardar.png)

:::caution[Before you save a time zone change]
Read the warning carefully: the change also affects the organizations that inherit your zone and recalculates report days, past days included.
:::

## Step 4: Save or discard your changes

- Click **Save** to apply the changes.
- Click **Reset** to discard them all and go back to the saved values.

While there are no changes, both buttons are disabled.

## Result

Your organization now has its country, time zone, reporting currency and communication language. Reports and analytics count days in the time zone you chose.
