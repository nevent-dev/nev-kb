In Settings → Users you give your team access and keep track of who has access today and whom you have invited. From this screen you invite **administrators** by link, follow the status of each invitation and manage the people who are already members.

In this guide you invite an administrator and check their invitation. About 5 minutes.

## Before you start

You need to be the **owner or an administrator** of the organization. If you are not, the dashboard will not let you invite anyone or remove anyone's access.

Keep in mind that a Nevent account can only belong to **one** organization. If the person you want to invite is already in another one, they cannot accept with that same account.

## Step 1: Open the users screen

In the side menu, go to **Settings** and, in the **Workspace** block, click **Users**.

The screen has two blocks:

- **Members:** the people with access right now. Each row shows the person (name and email), their role, their access, their last activity and the available actions.
- **Administrator invitations:** the invitations you have sent, with their status and the status of the email delivery.

![The Users screen of the DEMO FEST organization with the Invite administrator button at the top right, the Members table with six people (owner, administrators and one scanning staff user) and, below it, the Administrator invitations table with one accepted invitation](/images/guias/organizacion/invita-a-tu-equipo/paso-1-miembros-e-invitaciones.png)

The **Last activity** column is the most useful one for reviewing access: it tells you whether someone has never signed in (“Never”) or when they last did.

## The three roles

| Role | For whom | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Whoever created the account | The original role. It cannot be assigned from this screen |
| **Administrator** | Your marketing and operations team | Full access to the organization's back office. It is the only role you can invite |
| **Scanning staff** | Door staff on the day of the event | They only scan the resources you authorize: tickets, add-ons or personalizations |

## Step 2: Invite an administrator

Press **Invite administrator**, at the top right. The window of the same name opens.

Type their **email** and confirm with **Send invitation**. The role is always **Administrator**: there is nothing else to choose.

![The Invite administrator window with the text “They will get an email with a link to join DEMO FEST. They have no access until they accept it”, the empty Email field, the Administrator role card and the Cancel and Send invitation buttons](/images/guias/organizacion/invita-a-tu-equipo/paso-2-dialogo-invitar-administrador.png)

The person receives an email with a link to join your organization. **They have no access until they accept it.** When they do, they disappear from Administrator invitations and move to Members.

If there was already a pending invitation for that email, the dashboard tells you and you can resend it from its row. If that person is already part of your team, it tells you too.

## Step 3: Follow the invitation's status

The **Administrator invitations** block shows each invitation with who sent it and when, and two status columns. Press the information icon next to each status to see what it means.

![The Administrator invitations table with one invitation in Accepted status and email delivery In the mail queue; the open information popover explains that the notice has moved to the outgoing mail queue and that delivery to the recipient's server is not confirmed](/images/guias/organizacion/invita-a-tu-equipo/paso-3-invitaciones-estado-envio.png)

The **Status** column tells you where the invitation stands:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | It is still open and can be accepted until its deadline |
| **Accepted** | The person who received it has joined the organization |
| **Voided** | Someone on your team voided it and its link no longer works |
| **Declined** | The person who received it declined it and it cannot be reopened |
| **Expired** | Its time ran out. You can invite this person again |

The **Email delivery** column tells you what is known about the notice email. It is information from the email provider, not about the person: “Delivered to the recipient's server” means the recipient's server accepted the notice, not that anyone has opened it. If you see **Bounced** or **Rejected by the provider**, check the address before resending.

While an invitation is **Pending**, its row lets you:

- **Resend**, which generates a new link. The previous one stops working.
- **Revoke**, which invalidates the link. It asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.

When an invitation has **Expired**, you have **Invite again**.

If you have just sent an invitation and do not see it updated, press **Refresh** in the block header.

## Step 4: Change permissions or remove someone

In the **Members** table, each row has its actions on the right:

- **Edit permissions**, only for scanning staff users. Check what they will be able to scan (**Tickets**, **Add-ons**, **Personalizations**), at least one option, and press **Save changes**.
- **Remove access**, which asks for confirmation before taking that person out of the organization. If you need them later, you will have to invite them again.

Your own row (marked **You**) and the owner's row do not have these actions.

:::note[Adding scanning staff]
From this screen you can only invite administrators. Scanning staff users who already exist still appear in Members with their permissions, and you can edit them or remove their access.
:::

Review this list after each event: door staff are usually temporary and it makes no sense for them to keep access until the next one.
