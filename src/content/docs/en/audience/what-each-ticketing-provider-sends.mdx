This page summarizes, provider by provider, what information reaches your Nevent audience when you connect a ticketing provider or a cashless integration.

Use it to know in advance what you will be able to segment and what not yet, depending on who sells your tickets.

![The "Add integration" window in Settings → Integrations with the catalog of available providers: Enterticket, Covermanager, DICE, Fourvenues, Onebox and CASFID, each with its "Configure" button](/images/guias/audiencia/que-datos-envia-cada-ticketera/paso-1-catalogo-integraciones.png)

## What each provider sends

| Provider | Category | Ticket purchases | Buyer data | Event and ticket type | Abandoned cart | Purchase UTMs | Cashless spending |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Onebox | Ticketing | Yes | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | No |
| Fourvenues | Ticketing | Yes | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | No |
| Enterticket | Ticketing | Yes | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | No |
| Covermanager | Ticketing | Yes | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | No |
| DICE | Ticketing | Yes | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | No |
| XCEED | Ticketing | Yes | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | No | Not confirmed | No |
| Fever | Ticketing | Yes | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Yes | Not confirmed | No |
| CASFID | Cashless | No | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not applicable | Not applicable | Yes |

:::note[How to read "Yes" and "No"]
A "Yes" or a "No" only appears when we can back it up with Nevent's documentation. Each provider's category (Ticketing or Cashless) determines whether it brings ticket purchases or cashless spending: a Ticketing provider brings you sales and attendees, not spending inside the venue, and the other way round with CASFID.
:::

## The only confirmed fact about abandoned carts

Today, of the providers in this table, only Fever sends the abandoned cart event to Nevent. XCEED does not send it. For the other providers we do not yet have confirmation of whether they send it: it shows as "Not confirmed" until it is verified case by case.

## If your ticketing provider is not on the list

If your ticketing provider does not appear in the table above, that does not mean you cannot use Nevent. See the guide [How to move forward if your ticketing platform isn't integrated](/en/audience/ticketing-platform-not-integrated/) to learn how to import your contacts in the meantime and how to request the integration you are missing.
