By the end of this tutorial you'll have created your first Magic Link, shared it (even if just as a test), and know where to check how many people clicked it. All in under ten minutes.

You don't need to have used Nevent before today. You just need access to your organization's admin panel.

## What a Magic Link is and why it's worth using

When you share a long URL, like `https://demofest.nevent.es/event/demo-fest-2026-entradas`, two bad things happen: it looks messy in your Instagram bio or on a poster, and you don't know how many people opened it.

A Magic Link solves both problems. Nevent generates a short link, like `nevt.link/2h92bh`, that redirects to your long URL and counts every click. That way you can find out whether the people who saw your post actually did something.

In this tutorial you'll create a manual Magic Link, meaning one that you decide when and where to use. There's no need to attach it to any campaign.

## Before you start

You need a real URL you want to send people to. It could be your event's tickets page, your social media profile, or any other web address. Have that URL ready before you continue.

## Step 1: Open the Magic Links section

In Nevent's side menu, find the **Tools** section and click **Magic links**.

A table opens with all the Magic Links in your organization. If you've just started, the list might be empty or contain only links generated by the platform. Don't worry: you'll add your own in the next step.

Take a look at the table's columns. You'll use them later to read your data:

- **Destination**: the title you set, or the destination URL if you didn't set a title.
- **Source**: shows whether you created the link manually, whether it was generated by a campaign, or by the assistant.
- **Link**: the short `nevt.link` code.
- **Total clicks**: how many times someone opened that link.
- **Used in**: how many campaigns that link appears in.
- **Last click**: when someone last clicked it.

## Step 2: Create the Magic Link

Click the **+ Create Magic link** button, in the top right corner of the screen.

A form opens. Fill in the two main fields:

1. **Destination URL** (required): paste the long URL you want to redirect to here. It must start with `https://`. For example: `https://demofest.nevent.es/event/demo-fest-2026-entradas`.

2. **Title**: write a name that will help you recognize this link later. Be specific: include the channel where you'll use it and the event. For example: "Tickets Demo Fest 2026 - Instagram Bio." Once you have twenty links in the list, a title like that will save you time.

The other fields (expiration date, folders, active URL toggle) are optional and you can leave them as they are for now. The link works without touching them.

Once you've filled in the destination URL and the title, click **Create Magic link**.

## Step 3: Find your new link in the list

After creating the Magic Link, you're taken back to the list. Your new link appears in the table.

In the **Link** column you'll see something like `nevt.link/2h92bh`. That's your short URL. The part after the slash (`2h92bh`) is your link's unique code.

To build the full URL you'll share, add `https://` in front: `https://nevt.link/2h92bh`.

Copy that URL and paste it wherever you want: in your Instagram bio, in a WhatsApp message, in the body of an email, or in the description of a YouTube video.

## Step 4: Test the link

Before sharing it widely, check that it works. Open your browser, paste the short URL into the address bar, and press enter. You should land on the destination page you set.

That click you just made has already been counted. You'll see it reflected in the next step.

## Step 5: Check the clicks

Go back to **Tools > Magic links** in the Nevent panel.

Find your link in the table. In the **Total clicks** column you should now see at least 1 (the one you just made yourself while testing).

For more detail, click the **Expand detail** button that appears at the end of your link's row. This unfolds more information about that link.

You can also click your link's title to open its full profile. Inside you'll find:

- The **Summary** section, with the all-time total of clicks, the short URL, and the destination URL.
- The **Analytics** section, with time-range filters (last 24 hours, last 7 days, last 30 days or last 90 days) and country and device data.

## Result

You now have a working Magic Link. Every time someone opens that link, the counter goes up. You don't need to do anything else for the data to come in.

From now on you can create one link per channel (one for Instagram, another for email, another for the event poster) and compare which one gets the most response from your audience. With that information, you can distribute your next campaign where it actually works.
