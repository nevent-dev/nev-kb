This page is the quick reference to what each Nevent module brings and how its tiers differ. Use it to check whether a specific feature is in your plan or belongs to a higher tier.

Nevent is organized in three modules (Marketing, Experience and Revenue) and, within each one, in tiers. It is not a pricing guide: the exact cost of your plan is in the Billing center, or you can ask the sales team.

## How the tiers differ

The criterion is always the same, whatever the module:

| Tier | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Basic** | You have access to the feature, with a cap meant for trying it and using it at moderate volumes. |
| **Pro** | The platform works for you at a larger scale: it automates, predicts or acts without you having to be there, with higher limits. |

No capability disappears completely on Basic if it can be offered with a reasonable cap. What changes between tiers is mainly the volume and how much the platform does automatically.

## Revenue

Multichannel attribution and advanced analytics.

:::note[Availability]
Revenue is not sold as a standalone module yet. It is planned for 2027.
:::

| Feature | Single tier |
|---|---|
| Advanced tracking and Magic Links with UTM tracking | Included |
| Full analytics: events, audience, campaigns and deliverability | Included |

In the meantime, part of this analytics is already available in your current plan. See [understanding your analytics](/en/analytics/understanding-analytics/) to find out what you can access today.

## Across everything: included in any module and tier

Three things do not depend on the module or the tier you have:

| Item | Scope |
|---|---|
| Nevent AI | Natural-language query layer with Claude or ChatGPT. Included at no cost on any plan. |
| Contacts | Unlimited. |
| Segments | Unlimited. |

More about Nevent AI in [what Nevent AI is](/en/nevent-ai/).

## Limits per tier

These are the caps that do vary between Basic and Pro in the Marketing and Experience modules:

| Item | Basic | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Custom fields | 3 | 25 |
| Ticketing provider integrations | 1 | 3 |
| Audiences synced to Meta | 3 | 25 |
| Lookalike audiences | 1 | 10 |
| AI chatbots | 1 | 3 |
| Credits included per month | 1,000 | 3,000 |

Contacts, segments, users and history are not in this table because they are unlimited on both tiers.

To learn what a credit is and how it is used, see [how the plan and credits work](/en/usage/how-credits-work/).

## Add-ons

Extra capabilities you can add to your plan beyond what is included:

| Add-on | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Additional brand | One more brand in your organization. The first brand is included in the plan. |
| Additional ticketing provider | One more integration beyond those included in your tier. |
| Custom short URL | Your own domain for your short links. |
| Dedicated email IP | Advisable only from high monthly sending volumes. Quoted case by case. |

The cost of each add-on is available in the Billing center or from Nevent's sales team.

## Enterprise

Above Pro there is a tailored Enterprise tier, which includes support coverage for the whole season, a direct channel, an assigned Customer Success and quoted onboarding. See [support by plan](/en/usage/support-by-plan/) for the coverage details.

## Where to check your account's plan and price

This page describes the functional scope of each module and tier, not the price. To see your plan, its status and your account's monthly price:

- Your plan and its price are in **Settings → Account & plan → Billing center**. See also the [plan and billing reference](/en/usage/reference/).
- Changing module or tier, and the price of an add-on, are handled by Nevent's sales team.
